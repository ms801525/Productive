No account · No setup

Just open it and plan.

I left out sign-ups and passwords on purpose. Productive is meant to be as simple as possible: nothing to create, nothing to set up, nothing to remember. Open the page and start planning.

  • Saves automatically Your week is saved right here in your browser. Close the tab, come back later, and it's all still there.
  • Fresh every week Everything clears at midnight on Saturday. No backlog, no clutter, just a clean start each Sunday.
  • Private by default No email, no password, no tracking. Your tasks never leave this device.
