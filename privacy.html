1Who we are

Productive ("Productive", "we", "us") is a weekly planner web app made and run by Mustafa Ali from Halifax, Nova Scotia, Canada. This policy explains what information the app handles when you use it and what happens to it.

2What's stored, and where

Productive has no accounts and works entirely in your web browser. This is a deliberate choice to keep it simple and private. Everything below is saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. It isn't sent to us, and we have no way to see it.

InformationWhy
Your tasks: title, day, time, status, category, priority, location and notesTo show your weekly planner.
Your categories: names and colorsTo color-code your tasks.
Display preferences: filters, whether earlier days are shown, whether you dismissed the welcome messageSo the app looks the way you left it.
Error details: the last 10 technical error messages, the time, the page and your browser typeSo you can choose to report a problem. They stay on your device unless you report one.

Because this information is stored on your device, anyone who uses your device and browser can see it. If you share a device, keep that in mind before adding private details to your tasks.

3What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-ups or email addresses.
  • No cookies.
  • No analytics or tracking tools.
  • No advertising.
  • We never sell, rent or share your information. We don't have it to begin with.

4Third-party services

A few outside services are involved in delivering the app. They receive basic technical information (such as your IP address and browser type) when your browser connects to them, under their own privacy policies:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security and to run its service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Google Fonts supplies the app's typefaces, so your browser requests font files from Google's servers. See the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.
  • Google Maps is used only if you tap a task's location. That opens Google Maps with the location text you entered. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Email is used only if you contact me. The contact form doesn't send or store anything itself. It opens your own email app (or Gmail, Outlook or Yahoo Mail) with your message filled in, and you choose whether to send it. Your message then goes to my Yahoo Mail inbox, and I use your email address only to reply. See the Yahoo Privacy Policy.

5How long it's kept

  • Each week's tasks are deleted automatically at Saturday midnight (your local time).
  • Tasks planned for future weeks are kept until that week ends.
  • Categories and preferences are kept until you clear your browser's data.
  • Error details: only the 10 most recent are kept.
  • In a private or incognito window, everything is deleted when you close the window.

6Your choices and rights

  • See your data: everything stored is visible in the app.
  • Delete your data: clear this site's data in your browser settings. This removes all tasks, categories, preferences and error details from that device. If the app ever shows its error page, you can also download a backup or reset the app from there.
  • Stop using it: you can stop at any time. Nothing is left anywhere except on your own device.

Since we don't collect or hold any of your personal information, there's nothing for us to access, correct or delete on your behalf. If you have a question about your privacy rights, see Contact.

7Children

Productive isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone. All data stays on the user's device.

8Changes to this policy

If Productive changes how it handles information, for example by adding accounts or paid plans, this policy will be updated before the change takes effect, and the date at the top will change. Features that collect new information will tell you clearly before you use them.

9Contact

Questions about this policy or your privacy? Send me a message or email me.
