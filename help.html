How to use Productive.

Everything you need to plan your week, in about five minutes. Tap a topic to jump straight to it.

1Quick start

Productive is a weekly planner. Each week, Sunday to Saturday, is its own clean page. Every day has three columns: Create (planned), In progress and Done.

  1. 1 Add a task Click New task at the top, or the lime + button on your phone. Give it a name and pick a day.
  2. 2 Move it along Drag the card to In progress, then Done. Or tap the arrow on the card.
  3. 3 Start fresh At Saturday midnight the week clears. Sunday starts empty, with no backlog.

No sign-up, no setup, and everything saves automatically. There's no account to create and no save button. Reload, close the tab or restart your browser and your week is still there.

3Adding tasks

There are four ways to add a task:

  • Click New task in the top bar (desktop).
  • Tap the lime + button in the bottom-right corner (phone).
  • Click + Add on any day, or Add a task in an empty Create column. The day is filled in for you.
  • Press N on your keyboard.

4Moving tasks

On a computer

Click and drag a card into another column, or onto a different day. The column lights up lime when you can drop it.

On a phone or tablet

Press and hold a card for a moment until it lifts, then drag it. Drag near the top or bottom of the screen to scroll, or toward the left or right edge to slide between columns. A quick swipe just scrolls the page as normal.

The arrow button

The round arrow on each card moves it one step: Create → In progress → Done. On a Done task it reopens it back to Create. On phones the arrow is always visible; on a computer it appears when you hover over the card.

From the edit window

Open a task and change its Status or Day, then save.

5Editing & deleting

  • Edit: click or tap any card to open it. Change anything and press Save changes.
  • Delete: open the task and press Delete. An Undo button appears at the bottom of the screen for a few seconds in case it was a mistake.

6The weekly reset

This is the heart of Productive: no backlog, ever. Each week runs from Sunday 12:00 AM to Saturday 11:59 PM.

At Saturday midnight…
The week that just endedDeleted permanently, finished and unfinished tasks alike.
Tasks you planned for future weeksKept. Next week becomes "This week".
Your categories and settingsKept.
  • The countdown in the top bar (or under the progress bar on phones) shows exactly how long is left.
  • You can look ahead to future weeks, but never back at past ones.
  • Tip: if something must happen next week, use the → arrow next to the week dates to go to next week and add it there before Saturday.

Don't keep important records here. Because each week is deleted on purpose, Productive is for planning, not for storing things you need to keep.

7Overdue tasks

If a task isn't marked Done by its time, it moves to the next day at the same time. It keeps moving each day until Saturday.

  • A task with a time is overdue once that time passes.
  • A task without a time is overdue at midnight at the end of its day.
  • Done tasks never move.
  • Moved tasks show a lime "from Tue" badge so you know where they started.
  • On Saturday there's nowhere left to go, so the task stays and shows Overdue until the weekly reset.

8Categories & priority

Categories

Productive comes with a few starter categories (Work, Gym, Miscellaneous, Appointment, Personal, Study), but you can make your own with any name and color.

From the category row: tap + New category at the end of the row of category chips above the days (on phones it's the + New chip pinned on the right). Name it, pick a color, and it appears as a new chip you can filter by.

While adding a task: tap + New category under the category list. Name it, pick a color, and tap Add category. It's selected for that task straight away.

Click Categories in the top bar (the four-dot button on phones) to:

  • Change a color: tap its round color swatch.
  • Rename: type over the name.
  • Create your own: type a name (like Kids, Side project or Errands), pick a color from the swatches (or tap the rainbow + for any color), and press Add category.
  • Delete: tap the trash icon. Its tasks move to Miscellaneous so nothing is lost.

9Weeks, days & filters

  • Change week: use the arrows beside the dates at the top. On larger screens, Today brings you back to this week.
  • Jump to a day: tap a day in the strip of dates (Sun–Sat). Today is highlighted in lime.
  • Earlier days: days already past this week are folded into one bar. Tap Show to open them.
  • Filter: tap category chips (Work, Gym…) to show only those tasks. Tap All to clear.
  • Progress: the bar and the To do / In progress / Done counts show how your week is going.

10On your phone

  • Add tasks with the lime + button in the bottom-right corner.
  • Each day's three columns sit side by side. Swipe left on a day to see In progress and Done.
  • Forms slide up from the bottom. Tap outside them or the × to close.
  • Press and hold a card to drag it (see Moving tasks).

Add it to your home screen

Productive can sit on your home screen and open full screen, just like an app:

  • iPhone (Safari): tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android (Chrome): tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen or Install app.

Your phone and computer have separate lists. Tasks are saved in the browser you use, so tasks added on your phone won't appear on your laptop. Add Productive to your home screen so it's one tap away (see Bookmark it).

11No account needed

There's no sign-up, no password and nothing to set up, on purpose. Productive is built to be as simple as possible: open the page and plan.

  • It saves automatically in your browser, on your device. Reload, close the tab or restart your computer and your week is still there.
  • It resets every week. Saturday at midnight the finished week is cleared, so there's never a backlog to manage.
  • It's private. No email, no tracking. Your tasks never leave your device.

Use the same browser each time. Each browser and device keeps its own planner. Tasks you add in Chrome on your laptop won't show up in Safari on your phone.

14FAQ

Where did my tasks go?

If it's a new week, the previous week was cleared at Saturday midnight. That's the weekly reset, and it's on purpose. If tasks disappeared in the middle of the week, your browser's data may have been cleared (see Troubleshooting Contact me).

Why did my task move to another day?

It wasn't marked Done by its time, so it moved to the next day at the same time. See Overdue tasks.

Can I see last week?

No. Past weeks are deleted so every week starts clean. You can look at any week ahead.

Why can't I add a task to an earlier day?

Days that have already passed this week are locked. Add the task to today or a later day instead.

It says "That time has already passed". What do I do?

You picked today with a time that's already gone. Choose a later time, another day, or leave the time empty.

Why is there no sign-in?

To keep things simple. No account means nothing to set up, no password to forget, and no personal data stored anywhere but your own device. Open the page and plan.

Do my tasks sync between my phone and computer?

No. There's no account, so each browser keeps its own list. That's the trade-off for zero setup and complete privacy. Pick the device you plan on most and bookmark it there.

Is my data private?

Yes. There's no account, and your tasks are stored only on your device. Nobody else, including the developer, can see them. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete all my data?

Clear this site's data in your browser settings (often under Privacy → Site data or Cookies and site data). This removes every task, category and setting on that device.
